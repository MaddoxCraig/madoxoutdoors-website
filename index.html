What We Do

Hardscape & Landscaping Services in Lakeville, MN

From paver patios and retaining walls to seasonal property maintenance, we handle every phase of your outdoor project — design, installation, and cleanup.

Hardscaping — Patios, Driveways & Walkways

We design and install paver patios, paver driveways, and walkways built to last through Minnesota's freeze-thaw cycles. Every project is graded and compacted to prevent shifting and settling over time.

Retaining Walls & Structural Hardscape

Our retaining walls are engineered to hold ground and look great doing it. Using natural stone and quality block materials, we build structures that add curb appeal while protecting your yard from erosion.

Custom Landscaping & Seasonal Cleanups

We design landscapes that reflect your style and suit your property. Services include planting, edging, mulching, and spring and fall cleanups to keep your yard looking sharp year-round.

Drainage & Grading Solutions

Standing water and poor drainage damage your lawn and foundation. We install French drains, dry river beds, and proper grading to protect your property and put water where it belongs.

Snow Removal — Residential & Commercial

Season-long snow removal contracts for homeowners and businesses in Lakeville and the south metro. Emergency service available. We keep your driveways, walkways, and parking lots clear all winter.

Seasonal Property Maintenance

Our full-service property maintenance includes spring and fall cleanups, pruning, trimming, small tree removal, and full debris haul-away. We leave your property looking professionally maintained after every visit.
